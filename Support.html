Frequently Asked Questions

How do I search?
Enter a recipe name, ingredient, or instruction in the search box on the home page.
How do I add a recipe?
Complete every field in the Add Your Own Recipe form and select Add Recipe.
Why does the site remember my login?
The class project stores a simple login flag in localStorage so returning from this page opens the home screen.
Is this a secure production login?
No. A production website should use server-side authentication, password hashing, HTTPS, and secure sessions.
